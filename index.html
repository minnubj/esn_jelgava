JELGAVA · LATVIA · LBTU

Where Erasmus
becomes Jelgava.

We're the local ESN chapter at the Latvia University of Life Sciences and Technologies — the people who meet you at the station, translate the bus app, and drag you to the palace for a picnic when you'd rather stay in. About 250 international students pass through here every year. This is where they find each other.

ABOUT US

A local network for
a global semester.

ESN Jelgava is part of the Erasmus Student Network, a non-profit run by students, for students. We welcome every exchange and international student who lands in Jelgava — helping with arrival, integration, and the everyday questions Google can't quite answer. In between, we organise the trips, dinners, and quiet-café evenings that turn a semester abroad into a place you actually miss afterwards.

No membership fee decides who belongs here. If you've just arrived — or you arrived three years ago and never left — you're one of us.

250+ students / year
30+ nationalities
40+ events / year
16 active volunteers

"We're not a tour company. We're the friends you haven't met yet — we just happen to have a spreadsheet of bus times."
